# Simple-interest-calculator
A simple project that explains and calculates Simple Interest based on the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T) / 100

- **P (Principal):** The initial amount of money.
- **R (Rate):** The annual interest rate (in percentage).
- **T (Time):** The time period (in years).

## Example

If:
- **P** = 1000
- **R** = 5%
- **T** = 2 years

SI = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100
